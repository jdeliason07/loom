For creators

A bottle, sent free, with nothing owed. And if you decide to put it on camera, a link that pays you for what it sells.

The offer

We send you No. 01 — the whole thing, the bottle we sell for $49, not a sample version of it. It is yours. There is no contract to sign, no minimum number of posts, no approval to wait for, and no invoice if you never mention it. If it turns out not to be your sort of thing, keep it and say nothing. That is a real option and it costs you nothing to take.

If you do post it

You get a link of your own — vates.store/?ref=yourhandle — and a commission on everything it sells. The tag on that link is kept in the buyer's browser from the moment they first arrive, so a sale still counts for you if they think about it for a fortnight and come back on their own. It rides through the checkout and lands on the payment itself in our books, next to the money. That is deliberate: whoever pays creators here is reading the payments list, not an analytics dashboard that can quietly disagree with it.

Commission: — of every order the link produces, for as long as it keeps producing them.

What we ask

One thing, and it is not negotiable because it is the law rather than our preference: if you post about a bottle we gave you, say that we gave it to you, and if you are using a link that pays you, say that too. #gifted, #ad, "vates sent me this" — the wording is yours, the disclosure is not optional. It has to be visible without tapping "more", and a caption at the end of a description box does not count. This protects you considerably more than it protects us.

Beyond that: nothing. No brief, no talking points, no shot list, no draft for us to approve. If we wanted the video we would have made it ourselves, and it would have been worse.

Your video in our advertising

Separately, and only if you want to: we sometimes pay to run a creator's own video as an advert, or run it from your handle rather than ours. That is a different thing from posting on your own account, it is worth more than a commission, and it is not covered by us having sent you a bottle. There is a box for it below. Leaving it unticked changes nothing else about any of the above, and we will not ask again.

If you tick it we will come back to you with the terms — how long, which platforms, what it pays — before anything runs. Ticking a box on a web form is us being allowed to ask, not us being allowed to publish.

The same goes for your face

The front page carries a wall of creators who use the bottle. Every square on it is a silhouette and a made-up name today, and it says so underneath, because we will not imply an endorsement that nobody has given. A real name and a real portrait go up there when the person in them has said we may, and come down the day they say otherwise.

Send me one

Everything except the address is so we know who we are talking to. The address is so the parcel arrives.

Before you send this

Your address is used to post one parcel and nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list and it is not passed to anyone but the courier. Ask and it is deleted — Privacy.
